During Summer 2025, I studied abroad in Shanghai, China, living and studying at Shanghai International Studies University. The program centered around daily language practice and real-life interactions with native speakers, giving me the opportunity to apply what I learned in the classroom in meaningful, everyday contexts.

Beyond language, the experience exposed me to new cultures, perspectives, and ways of thinking. It was a humbling reminder of how big and diverse the world truly is, and it deepened my appreciation for stepping outside of my comfort zone.
